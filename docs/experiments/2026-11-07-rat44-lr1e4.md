## Reading

- **RAT is alive and the lower LR helps**: 1e-4 moves RAT44 from its 3e-4/22k
  **1.2911** to **1.2675 at 26.5k**. It is not a collapse.
- Against the **same-step** Real48 seed1338, RAT is still ~0.026 nats behind at
  26.5k. Against the earlier seed1337 the gap is much smaller at 22k
  (RAT 1.2793 vs Real 1.2816), so this is not yet a clean separation.
- Real seed spread is real: 1.2485 (seed1337) vs 1.2225 (seed1338), ~0.026. RAT
  needs a completed 28k point and a second seed before any architecture verdict.
- The RAT run is a timeout cut, not a failed result; the 26.5k checkpoint is valid.
