# RAT33/RAT22 at 1e-4 (2026-11-08) - 1e-4 is worse for RAT

The reverse of the Real48 result: lowering RAT's LR to 1e-4 **hurts** at both
widths. The 7.92 h timer did its job; both arms completed their 22k steps early.

## Results

| arm | lr | steps | train | val | note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| RAT33 (512x9 h64) old | 3e-4 | 22k | 1.2763 | **1.2540** | seed 1337 |
| RAT33 old | 3e-4 | 22k | 1.3033 | 1.2902 | seed 1338 |
| **RAT33 lower LR** | **1e-4** | **22k** | **1.2991** | **1.2874** | seed 1337 |
| RAT22 (416x9 h52) old | 3e-4 | 22k | 1.3036 | **1.3038** | seed 1337 |
| **RAT22 lower LR** | **1e-4** | **22k** | **1.3360** | **1.3199** | seed 1337 |

## Reading

- **RAT does not want the Real LR.** RAT33 goes 1.2540 -> 1.2874 and RAT22
  1.3038 -> 1.3199 when moving 3e-4 -> 1e-4. The 48M Real LR fix does not
  transfer; the two architectures have different optima.
- RAT33 is still the better RAT width: 1.2874 vs RAT22 1.3199 at matched LR and
  steps. The old RAT33 seed spread (1.2540/1.2902) is larger than the RAT22 gap.
- Fair comparison is now a **same-LR** comparison: Real48 1e-4 22k is 1.2816
  (seed1337) / 1.2528 (seed1338); RAT33 1e-4 1.2874, so RAT is close to one Real
  seed and behind the other. At RAT's own best LR (3e-4) RAT33 is 1.2540, which
  is near Real's better seed. Do not conclude either architecture wins from one
  seed.
- Next long run should use **RAT33 at 3e-4**, 2 seeds, longer steps, not 1e-4.
