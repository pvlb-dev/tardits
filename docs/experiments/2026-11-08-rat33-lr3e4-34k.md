# RAT33 3e-4, 34k, two seeds - it ties Real48

The full long run completed (no cancellation). RAT33 at its own LR (**3e-4**),
34k steps, two seeds, compared with Real48 at its tuned LR (**1e-4**), 36k,
two seeds. Same FineWeb data / block / batch, data-order fix, #23-clean source.

## Reading

- **At tuned LR and comparable budget, RAT33 and Real48 are tied on mean val**
  (1.2361 vs 1.2355, delta 0.0006). RAT33 does it with **31% fewer parameters**.
- The Real seed spread is ~0.026, much larger than the mean difference; RAT33's
  two seeds are almost identical (0.0007). So the honest claim is **parity /
  parameter efficiency**, not "RAT wins" or "Real wins".
- The RAT 1e-4 run remains the wrong-LR control (1.2874 at 22k); it is not part
  of this comparison.
- At 34k RAT33 improves from 1.2540/1.2902 at 22k to 1.2357/1.2364. The longer
  run matters.
- Runtime is comparable: RAT33 ~10.6 h, Real48 ~10.8 h.

## Fairness

Both architectures got an LR search now: Real 1e-4 was clearly better than
3e-4/5e-4; RAT 3e-4 was better than 1e-4. The seeds are matched; the token
budget is close: RAT 34k x 1024 x 4 = 139.3M chars vs Real 147.5M chars. This is a much fairer table
than the early Real-3e-4-vs-RAT-3e-4 pair.
